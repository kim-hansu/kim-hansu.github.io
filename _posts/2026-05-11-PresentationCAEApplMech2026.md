Prof. Hansu Kim and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RpcCyLQAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Sanghyuk Kim</a> gave presentations entitled "Design of an aircraft seat leg using topology optimization considering additive manufacturing constraints" and "Text-guided multiscale topology optimization for mechanical anisotropy design with TPMS," respectively, at the <a href="https://ksme.or.kr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">KSME CAE & Applied Mechanics Division 2026 Spring Conference</a>, held in Yeosu, Korea, while Minsang Cho and Geonung Jo also participated in the conference.
