Prof. Hansu Kim received a Certificate of Appreciation for Early Career Researchers from the <a href="https://ksme.or.kr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Korean Society of Mechanical Engineers (KSME)</a> in recognition of his invited presentation entitled “Design for Additive Manufacturing: From Industrial Challenges to Multi-material Decomposition Optimization” at the Early Career Researcher Session of the <a href="https://ksme.or.kr/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">KSME 2026 Conference</a>, held in Jeju, Korea.
