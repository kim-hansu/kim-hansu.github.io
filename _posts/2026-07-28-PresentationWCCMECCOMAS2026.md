Prof. Hansu Kim and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RpcCyLQAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Sanghyuk Kim</a> gave presentations entitled “Multi-material decomposition optimization for topology-optimized structures under additive manufacturing constraints” and “Text-guided multiscale topology optimization for mechanical anisotropy design with TPMS,” respectively, at the <a href="https://wccm-eccomas2026.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">17th World Congress on Computational Mechanics and 10th European Congress on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences and Engineering (WCCM-ECCOMAS 2026)</a>, held in Munich, Germany.

<details class="view-more">
   <summary>View More</summary>

   <p>
      Prof. Hansu Kim’s presentation was delivered in the mini-symposium 
      “Design and Topology Optimization for Multi-Component Systems,” 
      organized by Prof. 
      <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9nbcizgAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Il Yong Kim</a> 
      and Prof. 
      <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6SUrIQIAAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Markus Zimmermann</a>, 
      while Sanghyuk Kim’s presentation was delivered in the mini-symposium 
      “Agentic AI and Physics-Informed Machine Learning for Next-Generation Design and Manufacturing,” 
      organized by Prof. 
      <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=I5sAZ6cAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Seunghwa Ryu</a> 
      and Prof. 
      <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=GExK7EIAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Yuan Tong Gu</a>.
   </p>

   <p>
     WCCM is a biennial congress organized by the 
     <a href="https://iacm.info/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">International Association for Computational Mechanics (IACM)</a>. 
     The 17th WCCM was held jointly with the 10th ECCOMAS. The joint congress was co-organized by the 
     <a href="https://eccomas.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">European Community on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences (ECCOMAS)</a> 
     and the 
     <a href="https://www.gacm.de/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">German Association for Computational Mechanics (GACM)</a>.
  </p>
